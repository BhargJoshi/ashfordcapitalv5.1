Reasoning before the outcome is known.

Illustrative scenario, not a trade recommendation.

1 · Intelligence

An index at a range high. A policy decision in two days.

What matters

  • Rate expectations
  • Breadth of the advance
  • Behaviour at the high

What does not

  • Social-media targets
  • Unrelated headlines
  • Opinions without conditions

Output. A short list of variables and a view of the regime.

2 · Decision

The trader writes the decision down before acting.

Thesis
A close above the range, on broad participation.
Conditions
Breadth confirms. Policy does not surprise.
Alternative
A failed break and a return inside.
Conviction
Moderate. Event risk caps it.
Invalidation
A daily close back inside the range.

Output. A decision that can be tested, and exited without debate.

3 · Execution

Conditions are met, or they are not. The plan answers first.

  • Size comes from the distance to invalidation.
  • Enter only if the written conditions are met.
  • If hesitation appears, the plan decides.

Output. Action that matches the plan, whatever the mood.

4 · Review

Reasoning, execution and outcome are judged separately. What happened?
